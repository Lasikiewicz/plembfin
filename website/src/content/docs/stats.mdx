Stats reports only what Plembfin has recorded or imported. It cannot recover a play that
never reached the local archive.


## Reported totals


Stats can show:

- total watch count;
- unique movies and TV episodes;
- most-played movie and show leaderboards;
- movie-versus-TV comparisons;
- source-platform breakdown;
- first and most recent plays; and
- monthly activity.

## Change the report scope

Use the period picker for all time, a year, or a month. Then use the media filter for all
media, movies, or shows.

A rewatch is a separate event when it represents a genuine later play. Changing the report
scope changes the presentation only; it does not alter the archive.
