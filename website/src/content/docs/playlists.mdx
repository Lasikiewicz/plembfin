Playlists are ordered collections of movies and TV shows. A playlist can stay in Plembfin
only, or sync to any connected Plex, Emby, or Jellyfin server. Playlists never change
watched state.

## Open Playlists

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin Playlists page showing playlist rails with app icons and item counts"
  caption="Each playlist is a rail with its synced apps, item count, and Edit and Delete controls."
/>

Select **Playlists** in the sidebar. Each playlist is its own rail. The first four show
together; more playlists follow below. Old `/custom-lists` links open this page.

Beside each name, app icons show where the playlist syncs. Hover an icon for its status:
synced, waiting for the first sync, failed, or not found. **Plembfin only** means no app is
selected.

## Create a playlist

Choose **New playlist**, enter a name, and tick the apps under **Sync to apps**. Only
connected apps can be ticked; see [Settings → Media servers](/docs/settings/media-servers/).
Leave every app unticked to keep the playlist in Plembfin only.

## How app sync works

- Each app's copy holds only titles in that app's library. A title the app lacks stays in
  the Plembfin playlist with a **Missing from** note and joins the app's copy once it arrives.
- A TV show adds every episode the app has, in season and episode order. New episodes join
  on later syncs.
- The app playlists follow Plembfin's order.
- A Plembfin edit syncs a few seconds later. A background pass also runs every 5 minutes.

### Changes made in an app

Plembfin also picks up changes made to the playlist inside an app:

- A title added in the app is added in Plembfin and to the other selected apps.
- A title removed in the app is removed in Plembfin, but only while the app's library still
  has it. A title that left the library stays, marked **Missing from** that app.
- Removing one episode of a synced show keeps that episode out on later syncs.
- A playlist deleted in the app is deleted everywhere and moves to
  [Recently deleted](#recently-deleted).

A failed or empty read from an app never counts as a removal. When one sync would remove
many titles at once, or an app reports several playlists deleted at once, Plembfin
holds the change and shows a banner above the rail. Choose **Confirm** to apply it or
**Discard** to restore the app's copy.

## Add a movie or TV show

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/media-action-row-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin media detail action row with the playlist action"
  caption="The playlist action on a movie or TV detail page opens the playlist chooser."
/>

Use **Add to playlist** on a movie or TV detail page, or the **Add to playlist** submenu in
a poster card's menu. Pick a playlist, or choose **Create a new playlist**. The action reads
**In playlist** once the title belongs to one. A title can be in several playlists and also
in [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/) or [Ratings](/docs/ratings/).

## Reorder and remove titles

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-cards-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/playlists-cards-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin playlist cards with Earlier and Later buttons"
  caption="Earlier and Later move a title one place; the card menu removes it from the playlist."
/>

- **Earlier** and **Later** move a title one place in the playlist. They appear on full
  cards when the playlist has more than one title.
- **Remove from this playlist** in the card menu removes the title from this playlist only.
  Other playlists, Watchlist, Ratings, the library, and watch history stay unchanged.

## Edit or delete a playlist

- **Edit** renames the playlist or changes its apps. Unticking an app deletes the playlist
  from that app.
- **Delete** asks first, then moves the playlist to Recently deleted and deletes it from its
  synced apps.

## Recently deleted

Deleted playlists appear under **Recently deleted** at the bottom of the page, with who
deleted them (Plembfin or an app) and when.

- **Restore** brings the playlist back in Plembfin and in every app it synced to.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good. It cannot be undone.

## Privacy and watched state

Playlists are local choices. They never create watched events, change ratings, or change the
sync identity of a title. For the other private workflows, see [Watchlist](/docs/watchlist/)
and [Ratings](/docs/ratings/).
