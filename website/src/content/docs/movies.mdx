Movies is Plembfin's tracked movie library. It is built from records Plembfin has
recorded or imported, rather than being a raw file browser for every connected server.


## Find a movie

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/movies-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/movies-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin Movies library showing the search, sort, A–Z, poster size, and view controls"
  caption="Find a tracked movie with search, sorting, A–Z navigation, and progressive loading."
/>

1. Open **Movies** in the sidebar.
2. Enter a title or another available library value in **Search**.
3. Choose a sort order for title, watch date, or the available next-airing order.
4. Use the A–Z control to jump through the loaded results.
5. Adjust poster size or switch between poster grid, list view, and overview-card views.
6. Select a card to open its movie media page.

Large libraries load progressively as you scroll. Rewatches remain in the title's watch
history rather than creating a second movie card.

## Movie cards and quick actions

A movie card can show its poster, title, watch information, and the latest known sync or
availability state. These indicators describe the local record and can change after a
sync or metadata refresh.

On a desktop pointer, hover a poster card to reveal its three-dot button:

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/library-poster-menu-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/library-poster-menu-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin movie poster card with its three-dot menu open"
  caption="The redesigned card menu keeps date, matching, rating, list, and watch-state actions beside the poster."
/>

- **Edit watch date** inspects, changes, adds, or removes a recorded viewing date.
- **Fix match** corrects the provider identity attached to the movie.
- **Rate** sets a private local score without marking the movie watched or changing the provider rating.
- **Add to watch list** saves the movie for later.
- **Add to Custom list** opens the list chooser; the arrow indicates that more than one list can be selected.
- **Mark unwatched** clears the current watched state and propagates that correction.

The library refreshes in place after an action. Use the media page for artwork, trailers,
galleries, the full watch history, and title-scoped sync actions.
