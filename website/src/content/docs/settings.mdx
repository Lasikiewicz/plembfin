Settings is the administration area for connections, metadata, synchronization, backups,
diagnostics, and maintenance.

Each top-level Settings group has its own guide. Open a group below, then use the right-hand
page outline to jump between the sections on that page.

## Working in Settings

<ScreenshotFigure
  darkSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-index-dark.png"
  lightSrc="/assets/app-captures/settings-index-light.png"
  alt="Plembfin Settings landing page used to make and verify one configuration change"
  caption="Make one settings change at a time, wait for completion, and verify it in the affected page or Sync Activity."
/>

Make one change at a time. Wait for the save or operation status to finish, then verify the
result in the affected app view or in [Sync Activity](/docs/sync-tools/).

For destructive maintenance and recovery actions, create a backup first and review the
operation scope before confirming it. Use [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) when a
setting, connection, or maintenance operation does not behave as expected.
