TV Shows groups tracked episode records into series and adds season, progress, and
next-airing context.


## Find a show


1. Open **TV Shows** in the sidebar.
2. Search by show title or use the sort and A–Z controls.
3. Use **Hide Fully Watched** to remove shows with no remaining unwatched episodes.
4. Use **Hide Ended** to remove ended shows when focusing on continuing series.
5. Adjust poster size or switch between poster grid, list view, and overview cards.
6. Select a show card to open its TV show media page.

Large libraries load progressively as you scroll. On a show media page, season episode
details load when a season is expanded so the initial summary is not held up by every
season's episode payload.

A show card can include watched-versus-total progress, last-play information, and the next
airing date. The list view can expand a show into seasons and episodes, but the selected
show's media page is where title-level actions live.

Unaired episodes are not treated as completed watches by a bulk action. If the title,
episode count, or progress is wrong, correct the series identity with
[Fix Match](/docs/media-details/#fix-match) before changing a whole season.

The child [TV Shows media page](/docs/tv-shows/media-page/) guide is about one selected
series, including its header, seasons, episodes, progress, actions, detail sections, and
page-specific Appearance controls.

See [Media details and actions](/docs/media-details/) for the shared explanation of
watch dates, matching, artwork, and sync behavior.
